How Integrity Rental runs, in five cards

Everything the case will ask you comes from this page. It is a summary of the IRP job description for the Virtual Assistant Property Manager. You can reopen it anytime with the Study Guide button at the top.

1 Your role and your team

You are the VA Property Manager of Integrity Rental Properties (Suffolk and Hampton Roads, Virginia). You run the day to day on TurboTenant, the operations platform for communication, payments, maintenance and work orders.

  • Jose, internal trainer: he guides you through this case.
  • Esteban Herrera, lead property manager: reviews your work and approves repairs under $500.
  • William McClung, owner: the final word on every application, every eviction, and every repair of $500 or more.

2 The leasing pipeline, in order

  1. Read the lead card twice. Hunt for inconsistencies between fields.
  2. Phone interview BEFORE any tour. Verify income, occupants, pets and move in date, every single time.
  3. Tour for leads that clear the interview. Confirm in writing.
  4. TurboTenant application: $45 paid by the renter. It returns verified credit, income, evictions and background from TransUnion.
  5. Questionnaires AFTER the application: employer verification and prior landlord interview for your finalists.
  6. Comparison sheet: all applicants side by side, then your recommendation goes to Esteban.

3 The screening bar

  • Verified income of at least 3× the rent.
  • Credit score of at least 620.
  • Clean eviction history and solid references.
  • Apply the same written rules to every applicant. That consistency is what keeps IRP safe under fair housing law.
  • Self reported numbers are a story; the report is the truth. People round up, so you verify instead of trusting or punishing.
  • An ESA is not a pet: no pet fee and no pet rent, but the letter must be on file.

4 Maintenance, the IRP way

  • Triage every request with the 4 tier urgency system: Emergency (danger to life or property, act now), Urgent (active damage, same day response, vendor within 24h), Standard (2 to 5 business days), Low (schedule when efficient).
  • Photos first. Never dispatch a vendor blind, and never ask a tenant to repair anything.
  • Check TurboTenant twice a day, morning and evening.
  • After every repair: notify the tenant, close the work order, send the 1 to 5 satisfaction survey.

5 Authority levels: who decides what

  • Level 1, you: routine communication, triage, vendor dispatch under $500, notices, inspections, closing work orders.
  • Level 2, Esteban: approves repair estimates under $500, borderline screening calls, lease enforcement beyond the standard timeline.
  • Level 3, the Owner: ALL tenant application approvals, every repair of $500 or more, evictions, lease terminations. You recommend; you never approve.
  • Golden rule: when in doubt, document and escalate. Never commit money or make policy exceptions outside your level.
