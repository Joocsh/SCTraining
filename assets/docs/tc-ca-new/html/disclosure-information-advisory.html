2. Purpose of This Advisory

This Disclosure Information Advisory is intended to inform Seller(s) of their disclosure obligations under California law and to provide guidance on key topics that arise during a residential real estate transaction. This advisory is not a substitute for legal, tax, or other professional advice. Seller(s) are encouraged to consult with appropriate professionals regarding specific questions or concerns.

3. Property Condition Disclosures

California law requires Seller(s) to disclose all known material facts that may affect the value or desirability of the property. Key disclosure forms include:

Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS): Required by California Civil Code §1102. Seller must complete and deliver to buyer.
Seller Property Questionnaire (SPQ): Supplemental disclosure regarding property history and condition.
Natural Hazard Disclosure (NHD): Report identifying natural hazard zones (flood, fire, earthquake, etc.).
Lead-Based Paint Disclosure: Required for homes built before 1978. This property was built in 1961.
Note: This property was built in 1961 (pre-1978). Federal law requires a lead-based paint disclosure and the EPA pamphlet "Protect Your Family from Lead in Your Home" to be provided to all prospective buyers.
