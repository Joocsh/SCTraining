Your role at a glance

Design and optimize workflows and SOPs · Manage team performance and accountability · Oversee client relationships and service delivery · Build operational dashboards and reports · Identify bottlenecks and implement solutions.

1Building & improving SOPs
ONBOARDING SOPCreate a detailed SOP for onboarding a new virtual real estate assistant: pre-start prep, Day 1 orientation, Week 1 training milestones, tool access setup (CRM, email, PM), 30-day benchmarks, escalation contacts.
2Team performance reviews
90 DAY REVIEWWrite a 90-day performance review template for a virtual TC: core competencies, KPIs, communication skills, client feedback, areas of excellence, areas for improvement, 90-day goals. Include a 1 to 5 rating scale.
3Client reporting
MONTHLY REPORTWrite a monthly client performance report narrative. Our VA handled 12 transactions, 100% deadline compliance, 87 emails sent, 4 positive feedback mentions. Last month: 10 transactions. 200-word executive summary.
4Process improvement analysis
BOTTLENECKSI identified 3 bottlenecks: (1) agents slow to respond to document requests, (2) title companies miss deadline reminders, (3) new TCs take too long to learn intake. For each, suggest 3 actionable solutions a virtual team could implement.
5Meeting facilitation
OPS REVIEWCreate a 60-minute agenda for a monthly operations review. Attendees: Ops Manager, Team Leads, HR. Topics: KPI review, client feedback, staffing updates, process improvement, Q&A. Include time allocations.
