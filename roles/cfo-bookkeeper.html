Your role at a glance

Manage month-end bookkeeping and reconciliation · Draft financial memos and narratives · Communicate with vendors and investors · Build financial templates and checklists · Explain financial concepts to stakeholders.

1Financial document drafting
VARIANCE MEMODraft a Q1 budget variance memo. Budgeted $45,000 revenue, achieved $52,000 (15.6% above). OpEx $38,000 vs $35,000 budget (8.6% over). 200-word executive summary.
2Explaining financial concepts
EXPLAINExplain accounts receivable aging in plain English for a new bookkeeper. Include what it is, why it matters in real estate, and what action to take at 30, 60, and 90+ days overdue.
3Creating financial templates
MONTHLY CHECKLISTCreate a monthly bookkeeping task checklist. Week 1: reconcile bank accounts, review AP. Week 2: payroll, expense categorization. Week 3: AR follow-ups, vendor invoices. Week 4: month-end close, statement review.
4Vendor & client communication
PAYMENT EMAILWrite a professional email to a vendor with an outstanding invoice of $3,200 from 45 days ago. Acknowledge the oversight, confirm details, provide a payment date within 10 business days. Professional, apologetic tone.
5Financial report narratives
INVESTOR UPDATEWrite a brief investor update: NOI this quarter $128,000, up from $112,000. Occupancy 96%. One property had a $8,500 HVAC repair. Expect Q3 NOI between $130,000 to 135,000.
