Your role at a glance

Oversee all paperwork from executed agreement through closing · Coordinate with title/escrow, lenders, agents, inspectors and appraisers · Track critical dates and deadlines · Maintain transaction files and ensure compliance · Communicate professionally with all parties.

Market-specific TC Playbooks

Every state has unique contract forms, timelines, disclosures, and closing procedures.

TXTexas: TREC forms · Option Period · Title company closings
Texas elementWhat you need to know
Option PeriodBuyer pays an Option Fee for the unrestricted right to terminate within a set number of days (typically 5 to 10). Unique to Texas.
TREC FormsAll contracts use TREC approved forms. Primary: One to Four Family Residential Contract (Resale).
Earnest Money TimingEMD must be delivered to the title company within 3 business days of execution.
SurveyA new or existing survey is typically required, ordered early.
Closing AgentTitle companies handle all closings: attorneys not required.
HOA AddendumTREC HOA Addendum required for HOA properties; seller has 7 days to provide HOA documents.

Verified source: Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC)

The One to Four Family Residential Contract (Resale) is a TREC-promulgated form, and Texas requires the use of TREC-approved contracts and forms for most residential resale transactions. Under the current contract (Paragraph 5), earnest money and the Option Fee must reach the escrow agent within 3 days of the effective date (the deadline moves to the next business day if it falls on a weekend or legal holiday). The Option Fee buys the buyer an unrestricted right to terminate during the negotiated Option Period and is non-refundable regardless of outcome.

Not legal advice. Confirm current form versions and deadlines at trec.texas.gov or with a TX-licensed broker/attorney before relying on this for a live file.

Step by step (13 steps)

  1. Contract ratification & file setup: verify TREC form, confirm Option Fee & 3 day EMD deadlines, send welcome emails.
  2. Option Period (Days 1 to 10): confirm inspector scheduled; track expiration.
  3. Earnest money confirmation: confirm title company received EMD within 3 business days; get receipt.
  4. Title ordered: send executed contract; request commitment.
  5. Survey ordered: confirm existing or order new.
  6. Appraisal (if financed): track deadline; alert agent if value low.
  7. HOA documents: confirm resale certificate ordered; buyer has 3 days to review.
  8. Third party financing deadline: confirm lender approval.
  9. Option expiration / amendment: ensure executed amendment in place before option expires.
  10. Title commitment review: flag exceptions to agents.
  11. Final walkthrough: 24 to 48 hrs before closing.
  12. Closing Disclosure: confirm buyer received CD 3 business days before closing (if financed).
  13. Closing day: confirm time/location; verify wire instructions sent securely.

I'm [Name], your SkillCloud Transaction Coordinator. Immediate action items: • OPTION FEE: $[Amount] due to [Seller's Agent] by [Date] • EARNEST MONEY: $[Amount] due to [Title Co] by [Date] (3 business days) • HOME INSPECTION: schedule during your Option Period, expiring [Date]. Your Option Period gives you the unrestricted right to terminate for any reason until [Date].

CACalifornia: CAR forms · Disclosure package · Escrow closings
California elementWhat you need to know
CAR FormsStandard contracts use CAR forms. Primary: Residential Purchase Agreement (RPA).
Disclosure PackageSeller delivers TDS, SPQ and NHD within 7 days. Buyer has 17 days to review.
Natural Hazard DisclosureNHD report identifies flood, fire, earthquake hazards. Ordered from a third party.
Contingency PeriodsDefault: 17 day inspection, 17 day appraisal, 21 day loan. All actively removed in writing.
Closing AgentEscrow companies handle closings. Title and escrow often separate entities.

Verified source: California Civil Code §1102.3

Confirmed directly against the statute (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov): once a completed Transfer Disclosure Statement is delivered, the buyer has the right to cancel within 3 days after delivery in person, 5 days after delivery by mail, or 5 days after delivery as an electronic record, on top of the purchase agreement's own contingency periods. This is separate from, not a substitute for, the 17-day inspection contingency in the C.A.R. RPA.

Not legal advice. Confirm current statute text at leginfo.legislature.ca.gov and current form language with C.A.R. or a CA-licensed broker/attorney before relying on this for a live file.

Step by step (13 steps)

  1. Contract ratification & file setup: verify signed RPA & counters, note contingency deadlines, open escrow, send welcomes.
  2. Open escrow: send executed contract; confirm escrow number/officer; EMD within 3 business days.
  3. Disclosure package (Day 1 to 7): confirm TDS/SPQ/NHD prep; track 7 day delivery.
  4. Inspection contingency (17 days): buyer schedules; negotiate repairs; remove in writing.
  5. Appraisal ordered: track 17 day contingency; resolve low value.
  6. Loan contingency (21 days): confirm lender approval on track.
  7. Natural Hazard Disclosure: confirm delivered; buyer signs receipt.
  8. Contingency removal: request CR form once buyer satisfied.
  9. Preliminary title report: review for liens/issues.
  10. Loan docs to escrow: coordinate buyer signing appointment.
  11. Final walkthrough: 1 to 5 days before closing.
  12. Closing Disclosure: at least 3 business days before closing.
  13. Funding & recording: confirm wire/funding; recording confirms closing.

VAVirginia: NVAR/REIN forms · HOA/POA disclosure · Attorney or title closings
Virginia elementWhat you need to know
Contract FormsNorthern VA uses NVAR forms; Hampton Roads/Virginia Beach uses REIN forms.
HOA/POA DisclosureSeller orders the packet. Buyer has 3 days to review after receipt and may cancel.
Resale CertificateFor condos, seller provides a Resale Certificate; buyer has 3 days to review.
Closing AgentBoth title companies and attorneys can conduct closings.
Ratification DateAll timelines flow from the ratification date: the date the last party signs.

Verified source: Virginia Resale Disclosure Act (Code of Virginia) · Virginia DPOR

The Virginia Resale Disclosure Act consolidates the resale-certificate and disclosure-packet requirements of the Property Owners' Association Act, the Condominium Act, and the Real Estate Cooperative Act into one framework, so an HOA resale certificate and a condo disclosure packet follow the same buyer-review rule. The Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation (DPOR) Common Interest Community Board publishes the official resale certificate form.

Not legal advice. Confirm current requirements at dpor.virginia.gov and with a VA-licensed broker/attorney before relying on this for a live file.

Step by step (12 steps)

  1. Contract ratification & file setup: confirm ratification date; all deadlines flow from it.
  2. EMD delivery: typically 1 to 3 business days; confirm receipt.
  3. HOA/POA disclosure ordered: buyer has 3 days to review after receipt.
  4. Inspection period: typically 10 days.
  5. Inspection negotiation: use proper VA addendum; confirm seller response.
  6. Appraisal (if financed): track deadline.
  7. Title search ordered: review for issues.
  8. Financing contingency: confirm loan approval on track.
  9. Home warranty (if applicable): confirm ordered, share coverage.
  10. Final walkthrough: 24 to 48 hrs before closing.
  11. Closing Disclosure: 3 business days before (if financed).
  12. Closing day: verify wire securely; follow up on recorded deed.

NYNew York: Attorney driven · Co-op & condo boards · Longer timelines
New York elementWhat you need to know
Attorney RequirementNY requires attorneys for all closings: both buyer and seller.
Contract TimelineClosings typically 60 to 90 days. Contract not exchanged until attorneys review.
Co-op Board ApprovalBuyer must be approved by the co-op board: 4 to 8 weeks.
Condo BoardCondos have a right of first refusal; board must waive before closing.
Mortgage ContingencyStandard 60 days. Extensions common.
Mansion TaxPurchases over $1M subject to NY Mansion Tax (1%, paid by buyer).

Verified source: New York Real Property Law §462

Confirmed directly against the statute (nysenate.gov): RPL §462 requires the seller to deliver a completed Property Condition Disclosure Statement before the buyer signs a binding contract, and the statute is explicit that these are representations of the seller, not the seller's agent. Note: the "attorney review" practice itself is a longstanding market custom in NY, not a codified statutory period the way it is in some neighboring states, so contract language and local practice both matter here.

Not legal advice. Confirm current statute text at nysenate.gov and current practice with an NY-licensed attorney or broker before relying on this for a live file.

Step by step (11 steps)

  1. Accepted offer & attorney selection: exchange info; not binding until attorneys exchange signed contracts.
  2. Attorney contract review: seller's attorney drafts; buyer's reviews. 1 to 3 weeks.
  3. Contract signed & deposit paid: buyer pays 10% deposit, held in escrow by seller's attorney.
  4. Home inspection: often done before contract; if post contract, track deadline.
  5. Mortgage application: track 60 day mortgage contingency.
  6. Title search ordered: review for liens/encumbrances.
  7. Co-op/condo board package: 50 to 100+ pages; submit to managing agent.
  8. Mortgage commitment letter: share with both attorneys.
  9. Clear to close: title confirmed clear; attorneys coordinate date.
  10. Final walkthrough & closing funds: buyer arranges wire.
  11. Closing day: both attorneys present; keys on recording.

FLFlorida: FAR/BAR As Is · 15-day inspection · Estoppel certificates
Florida elementWhat you need to know
As Is ContractFAR/BAR As Is is most common. Seller not obligated to repair; buyer retains right to inspect and cancel.
Inspection PeriodStandard 15 days. Buyer can cancel for any reason with full EMD refund.
HOA EstoppelSeller obtains an Estoppel Certificate disclosing unpaid assessments/violations. Takes 10 to 14 days.
Flood ZoneSellers disclose flood zone status; review insurance, especially coastal.
Closing TimelineTypically 30 to 45 days from execution.

Step by step (13 steps)

  1. Contract ratification & file setup: confirm FAR/BAR As Is executed; note effective date.
  2. EMD: typically within 3 business days; confirm receipt.
  3. Open title/escrow: confirm officer and order number.
  4. Inspection period (15 days): track carefully; buyer can cancel for ANY reason.
  5. HOA estoppel ordered: 10 to 14 days; flag delays.
  6. Flood zone/insurance review: address early.
  7. Appraisal (if financed): track deadline.
  8. Loan approval / financing contingency: confirm lender on track.
  9. Title commitment: review Schedule B exceptions.
  10. Inspection period decision: confirm before expiry; if canceling, execute refund.
  11. Clear to close: title prepares settlement statement.
  12. Final walkthrough: 24 hrs before.
  13. Closing day: confirm wire; follow up on recorded deed.
