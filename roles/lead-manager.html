Your role at a glance

Manage inbound/outbound pipelines · Qualify leads via calls, texts, emails · Maintain CRM records and follow-ups · Coordinate handoffs to agents · Build and optimize nurture sequences.

1Lead outreach scripts
COLD OUTREACHWrite a cold outreach email to a homeowner who filled out a home valuation form on our site. Goal: get them on a call with one of our agents. Helpful, low pressure tone. Property in Virginia Beach, VA.
FOLLOW UP TEXTWrite a follow-up text for a lead who hasn't responded in 3 days. Under 160 characters. Re-engage without being pushy.
2Lead qualification scripts
QUALIFICATION CALLCreate a qualification script for discovery calls. Determine: timeline to buy/sell, motivation, financial pre-qualification status, current living situation, preferred neighborhoods. Format as a call guide with note fields.
3Drip email sequences
5 EMAIL DRIPCreate a 5-email nurture sequence for buyer leads 3 to 6 months out. 1: Intro. 2: Market education. 3: Buying process. 4: Testimonial/social proof. 5: CTA to schedule. Each 150 to 200 words.
4CRM note writing
CRM ENTRYClean up these call notes into a CRM entry: "talked to John, wants to sell in the fall, not sure about price, wife needs to agree, lives in Chesapeake, 3br 2ba, no agent yet." Format: Lead Summary, Key Details, Next Steps, Follow-up Date.
5Lead reporting
WEEKLY SUMMARYWrite a performance summary: 42 new leads, 18 contacted, 6 qualified, 2 appointments, 1 referral. Last week: 38 / 15 / 4 / 1. Highlight wins and areas to improve.
