Your role at a glance

Respond to inquiries and schedule showings · Screen applicants and verify documentation · Draft, review and manage leases · Coordinate move-ins and move-outs · Handle renewals and communicate with property managers.

1Responding to rental inquiries
EXAMPLE PROMPTWrite a professional response to a rental inquiry for a 2 bedroom apartment at 1204 Atlantic Ave, Virginia Beach. Monthly rent $1,850. Available June 1. Include a placeholder application link and note showings are by appointment.
2Applicant communication
CONDITIONAL APPROVALWrite an email to a rental applicant letting them know their application is approved pending verification of employment. Ask for last two pay stubs and an offer letter within 48 hours.
DENIAL LETTERWrite a professional denial letter for a rental application. Reason: insufficient income (did not meet 3x monthly rent). Be polite and do not elaborate on specifics.

Stay compliant

Denial letters touch fair housing and adverse action rules. Keep them factual, consistent, and reviewed by your manager before sending.
3Lease summaries & review prep
EXAMPLE PROMPTSummarize the key tenant obligations in the following residential lease. Highlight: rent due date, late fee policy, pet policy, maintenance responsibilities, and notice period for non-renewal. [Paste lease text]
4Move-in & move-out communication
EXAMPLE PROMPTDraft a move-in instruction email for a new tenant moving in June 1. Include placeholder for key pickup location/time, utility setup reminder, and a note about the move-in inspection checklist due within 48 hours.
5Lease renewal outreach
EXAMPLE PROMPTWrite a lease renewal offer to a tenant whose lease expires August 31. Offer a 12-month renewal at $1,950/month (up from $1,850). Friendly tone; emphasize the value of renewing.
