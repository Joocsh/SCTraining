Your role at a glance

Draft and manage client correspondence · Build meeting agendas and clean up notes · Create SOPs and process documents · Write listing captions and social media content · Build monthly content calendars for agents · Send email newsletters to past client lists.

Administrative use cases

1Drafting professional correspondence
EXAMPLE PROMPTDraft a professional email on behalf of our team leader to a client who missed their showing. Be polite, offer to reschedule, and suggest two slots: Tuesday 2pm and Thursday 11am.
2Meeting agendas & notes
AGENDACreate a structured agenda for our weekly team check-in. Topics: transaction pipeline, pending tasks, client feedback, upcoming deadlines, announcements. 30 minutes.
CLEAN UP NOTESHere are my rough notes from today's meeting. Clean them into a professional summary with action items, owners, and deadlines. [Paste raw notes]
3Creating SOPs & process documents
SOPCreate an SOP for how a TC at a real estate virtual staffing company handles a new transaction intake: from receiving the contract to setting up the file. Numbered checklist with brief descriptions.

Social media use cases

4Property listing captions
CAPTIONSWrite 3 Instagram captions for a 4 bedroom waterfront home in Virginia Beach listed at $875,000. Tones: excitement focused, lifestyle focused, investment focused. 5 hashtags each.
5Monthly content calendar
CALENDARCreate a 4-week social calendar for a real estate agent in Virginia Beach. Instagram + Facebook, 3 posts/week. Mix: market updates, listing highlights, testimonial prompts, community content, agent tips. Table format.
6Email newsletters
NEWSLETTERWrite a monthly newsletter for a team's past client list. Warm opening, 3 summer homeowner tips, a market update paragraph (placeholder data), CTA to refer friends. 300 to 400 words.
