Module 1

Understand SOPs

Foundation

A process tells the story. An SOP makes it repeatable.

Understanding the difference is the first step toward useful documentation.

1

See the difference

2

Read the definitions

Process

What happens

A process is a series of actions or steps taken to achieve a specific goal. It describes what happens and the order in which it happens.

Examples: checking and responding to client emails, onboarding a client, or preparing a weekly report.

SOP

How it happens

A Standard Operating Procedure is a detailed, written, step by step guide explaining exactly how to complete a process consistently and accurately.

It documents instructions, tools, responsibilities, and troubleshooting.

3

Compare them side by side

Quality writing

Write so a new teammate can succeed without guessing.

1

Make instructions actionable

Less direct

“It is required that the report be submitted by the VA.”

Clearer

“Send the report to the team lead.”

Use descriptive titles and store SOPs in an accessible, organized location.

2

Strong SOP habits

  1. Start with the end in mind: know why the SOP exists.
  2. Use simple, direct language, short sentences, and action verbs.
  3. Be specific and detailed without overwhelming the reader.
  4. Use numbered steps and bullets for scanability.
  5. Add screenshots, links, or visual aids where useful.
  6. Use a consistent format across SOPs.
  7. Review and test the SOP; ask someone else to follow it.
  8. Review periodically, every 6 to 12 months or after a system or tool update.

Official assessment guide

Your SOP assessment: follow the required order.

1

The four steps

  1. 1

    Review the VA SOP Handbook

    Begin by carefully reading it. It contains the process explanation, context, rules, expectations, roles, tools, timing, and exceptions. Quiz answers and SOP content must be based exclusively on the handbook.

  2. 2

    Complete the knowledge quiz

    This confirms comprehension. The assessment is not a memorization exercise; it evaluates process understanding, logical thinking, attention to detail, and standardized documentation.

  3. 3

    Create the Executive Assistant SOP

    Copy the official SOP Template and use that copy to build the “Executive Assistant” SOP from the Executive Assistant Handbook. Reflect the handbook completely, use the official structure, write clearly and professionally, and add no assumptions or external information.

  4. 4

    Export and submit

    Export the completed SOP as a PDF. Email it to ea.coaching@skillcloudstaffing.com with the subject line SOP Assessment – [Your Name]. Coaches review it and provide feedback.

2

How it is evaluated

Evaluation criteria

  • Accuracy and alignment with the handbook
  • Completeness: no missing sections
  • Clarity and ease of following
  • Proper use of the SOP template
  • Attention to detail: no invented steps or tools
  • Professional writing and formatting

Key rules

  • Do not copy text blindly; understand and rephrase appropriately.
  • Do not add steps, tools, or rules not in the handbook.
  • Use clear, professional English.
  • Follow the SOP structure exactly as provided.
