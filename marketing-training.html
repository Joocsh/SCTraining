Module 1 · The brand

What is Sotheby’s International Realty?

A premier luxury real estate brokerage founded in 1976, operating a global network of more than 1,100 offices across 84 countries.

The Art of Living: how the brand evolved

MID 1970s

A pattern at the auction house

Sotheby’s wealthiest clients were not only buying rare art and antiques. They were also buying and selling the grand estates required to house those collections.

1976

SIR is born as a subsidiary

The auction house launched Sotheby’s International Realty to serve its existing clientele, creating a seamless pipeline between fine art and luxury property backed by the trust of the Sotheby’s name.

~30 YEARS

A brokerage with limits

SIR operated primarily as a brokerage that owned its own offices, but opening physical locations everywhere required capital that limited its global potential.

2004

The franchise turn

Realogy (later Anywhere Real Estate) paid $100 million to license the SIR name for 50 years, with options to renew. Independent luxury brokerages worldwide could now rebrand as Sotheby’s International Realty: instant prestige of a 280 year old name, while staying locally owned.

EARLY 2026

Compass International Holdings

A landmark merger between Anywhere and Compass was announced, creating Compass International Holdings. Despite the consolidation, SIR keeps its distinct identity and operates as the ultra luxury crown jewel of the portfolio.

The current global standard

“1 of 1”

For several years SIR has used the 1 of 1 campaign as its global brand identity. It is not a temporary promotion: it is the permanent standard applied to everything, from local yard signs and social posts to high end print features in The New York Times and Architectural Digest.

The result: every property, at any price point or location, is positioned not as a listing but as a singular, irreplaceable piece within a curated global collection.

Your gate: SIR Access

Accessing the SIR platform is one of the first steps of the day. It is a gated environment where your role defines your toolkit: listing data, high end marketing, or global analytics, everything is available the moment you log in.

  • Secure login with two factor authentication.
  • Two main entry points: the SIR platform and Propel, the central hubs for communication and resources.
  • Links and resources inside Propel vary by client and account configuration.
Client permissions matter. The permissions your client grants may affect your access to these tools. Discuss access levels with them up front so your workflow never stalls.

Module 3 · Campaign playbook

“1 of 1”: the rules of the brand

The proposition: Curators of the unique. SIR connects the world’s most unique homes with the buyers who appreciate their potential, an authority for a new era of luxury defined by an eye for what makes each home, experience, and lifestyle special.

Messaging system

The lead campaign headline is always “1 of 1”. Around it, the system gives you four types of alternative headlines plus audience specific variants:

Headline typeWhat it referencesExample directions
UniversalWorks with any property shown.“The one for you”, “One of a kind”, “Your one awaits”, “Find your one”.
PropertySpecific features of the property shown.“The [style of home] one”: the midcentury one, the contemporary one, the townhouse one.
LocationThe locations shown.Anchor the headline in the neighborhood or city.
LifestyleThe lifestyle the property implies.Speak to the way of living the home unlocks.

Uniqueness copy reinforces the idea everywhere: curators of the unique, character as unique as yours, homes as unique as you, live unique, a genuine original, exquisite detail, uncommon beauty, true original, living masterpiece.

Audience specific angles: Buyers get unique access, sellers get unique appreciation, agents get unique advantage, and renters get their own variants. Each pairs with unique leadership plus a call to action.

Layout system

  • Layouts are a flexible system: visuals can reflect the era of each property and adapt to different amounts of information.
  • A centered, simple, classic layout appeals to the established core audience, clean and classical.
  • These are two ends of a spectrum, and you can blend elements of both.
  • Image scale: hero images take half the canvas width or more so they stay the most prominent element. Supporting images stay clearly smaller to build hierarchy.
  • Spacing: every element needs room to breathe, especially text in complex layouts.

Logo rules

  • Positioning: classic layouts center the logo at the bottom. Modern layouts place it in a corner.
  • Minimum distance: keep clear space equal to the height of the “S” around every edge of the logo.
  • On color: on SIR blue backgrounds the logo is pure white. On off white or pure white backgrounds the logo is SIR blue.
  • Affiliates: affiliate logos follow the same rules. Pick the lockup that best complements the application size, scale, and surrounding elements.

Module 4 · Tool deep dive

Propel: your daily hub

The primary hub where SIR agents collaborate, access marketing assets, and stay on top of local market trends. Everything from “Haves/Wants” forums to listing templates lives in one place so you spend less time hunting for tools.

How you use it

  • Navigate to the platform URL and log in with your company Gmail via Single Sign On. No new password required.
  • Use the Quick Links dashboard to jump straight into Design Vault, the training video library, SIR Access login, helpdesk tickets, referrals, one to one training, property journey videos, and the holiday schedule.
  • Join regional Community Forums to share off market listings and collaborate with colleagues by client location.
  • Manage meetings through the integrated calendar: upcoming meetings, company holidays, and professional development training.

Its two primary functions

  • Posting updates: agents share new listings, price adjustments, back on market notices, and open house details. Updates are organized by region (Vermont, New Hampshire, New York and so on) and are automatically emailed to the other agents in the office.
  • Training and resources: a hub for webinars, training videos, marketing materials such as flyers and postcards, and legal or accounting forms.

The Launch Pad gives one stop access to the rest of the stack: ActivePipe, MoxiWorks, Testimonial Tree, and Design Vault, plus a resource library with seller and buyer guides, the SIR Style Guide, and market reports.

Login note. Many tools inside Propel require two step verification. Keep your client’s or your own login credentials handy before you start a task.

Module 5 · Tool deep dive

ActivePipe: email that sells the listing

An enterprise grade email marketing platform. SIR uses it to provide branded templates that promote properties and programs to local markets, typically in the after launch phase, once a home is active on the market: listings, open houses, price adjustments, and events.

  • Dashboard: statistics on recent emails, campaign activity, contacts, and property notifications the moment you log in.
  • Email builder: drag and drop editor for titles, text, images, buttons, videos, and property listings.
  • Smart campaigns: automate follow up with drip campaigns and engagement triggers.
  • Contacts and audiences: add manually or import CSV files, then organize the database with tags and audience groups for targeted sends. It doubles as a database manager: distribution lists, categorized contacts, automated monthly newsletters.
  • Insight: by tracking which properties clients click, ActivePipe shows you the most active leads so agents focus their effort where it counts.
  • Listing integration: auto populate live listings with the property integration tools, cutting manual entry.
  • Preview and testing: always run “View Preview” for formatting, links, and mobile responsiveness, and use “Send Preview” to test internally before distribution.
  • Images: if uploads fail, resize and optimize files to platform requirements without losing quality.
  • Videos: linked videos must be public to display. Vimeo is often recommended for cleaner presentation.
  • Listing availability: new listings can take 1 to 2 days to sync. After that, add content manually or contact the office administrator.
  • Mobile formatting: review the mobile preview before sending. Side by side layouts may stack vertically on small screens.
Subscription. ActivePipe requires a paid subscription.
Login note. Marketing and admin accounts can manage most features without the agent’s login, depending on assigned permission levels.

A complimentary design tool for creating agency quality marketing campaigns in print and digital. Templates come preset to the brand’s aesthetics, so fonts, colors, and logo standards stay consistent while you personalize with listing photos, details, and contact info.

  • Workflow: prepare materials before a listing goes live, or update them after launch on client request.

  • Ordering prints: through the integrated print portal linked to a third party service.

Module 7 · Tool deep dive

ListTrac: proof the marketing works

The reporting and analytics platform of the network. It aggregates online traffic from thousands of websites to show how many potential buyers are viewing listings globally, and turns that into automatic seller reports.

What you can see

  • Dashboard: listing activity, website performance, visitor traffic, and engagement analytics in one place.
  • Listing statistics: views, inquiries, favorites, shares, and traffic across affiliated sites, including The Wall Street Journal, Zillow, and SIR.com, so you can spot which sites generate the most engagement.
  • Report history: confirms when reports were sent, delivered, and opened by sellers, keeping communication consistent and verifiable.

Seller reports

  • Create automated reports by selecting the listing, adding contact information, and customizing settings.
  • Schedule them weekly, bi weekly, or monthly with performance summaries, website traffic, and engagement statistics.
  • They prove the value of global syndication with concrete data on the home’s digital exposure, helping agents justify strategy and keep sellers informed with transparent, data backed updates.
  • Before activating reports: verify seller contact info, review scheduling, and preview the formatting.
  • Listings may take 3 to 5 days to appear after going live, and analytics may not update immediately.
  • Seller reports can land in spam or junk folders, and an incorrect seller email blocks delivery entirely.
  • Early listing activity shows limited statistics until data fully syncs.
Login note. Access via SIR Access. Admin and marketing users may have the same reporting and listing features as agents, depending on brokerage assigned permissions.

MoxiWorks: win the listing before it exists

The premier presentation and business suite of the network. Visually stunning, data driven presentations that showcase market expertise and the power of the global brand in a digital first format, plus integrations with RealScout and Testimonial Tree for lead engagement and marketing support.

Building a presentation

  • Dashboard: CMA presentations, templates, property data, client marketing tools, and your saved drafts, always editable.
  • Start with Create New, choose a CMA or listing presentation template, and let MLS data streamline the setup.
  • Enter an MLS number for a previously listed property and photos, descriptions, and tax information can populate automatically.
  • Decks are interactive and web based, looking exceptional on any device from tablets to large screens.

Market comparisons & output

  • Add comparable properties from active, pending, withdrawn, or sold listings to frame an accurate price range.
  • Side by side comparisons make pricing strategy and market trends clear to clients.
  • Customize by removing pages or adjusting sections, then preview before sending: formatting, property summaries, comparisons, preparation checklists.
  • Share the finished deck with clients. Drafts save automatically for future edits.
  • Verify MLS information, review comps carefully, and confirm pricing recommendations before finalizing a CMA.
  • Outdated MLS or tax information may need manual updates, and an incorrect MLS number blocks property data.
  • Missing photos usually mean MLS syncing is delayed.
  • Customizing pages creates a cleaner, more personal client experience. Review formatting before sending, every time.
Login note. Access via SIR Access. MoxiWorks may require two step verification, and sign in works with username or email, Google, or a Microsoft account.

How to structure a prompt: five ingredients

Five ingredients turn a vague request into a great response. The key insight: Claude works best when it understands who it is talking to, what the situation is, and what success looks like. Most people skip those details and then wonder why the output feels generic.

1 · Role2 · Context3 · Task4 · Format5 · Example
  • Role: tell Claude who to be, its expertise, tone, and perspective.
  • Context: share the background, who the audience is, what the situation is.
  • Task: state clearly what you want done, with active verbs.
  • Format: specify length, structure, and style: bullet list, paragraphs, table.
  • Example: optional but powerful, show Claude a sample of what good looks like.

There is a full “Prompting 101 with Claude” session in video inside the original deck, plus complete example outputs stored as course material in Google Classroom.

The prompt library, by role

Each template below follows the five ingredient structure, color coded. Copy the skeleton, replace the bracketed fields, and adjust to your case. The “key move” explains why each one works.

RoleContextTaskFormat
Act as a senior graphic designer specializing in real estate brand identity. I am attaching a post I designed for a new listing. Our brand guidelines specify: primary colors [HEX + HEX], typography [DISPLAY FONT] for headlines and [BODY FONT] for body, a clean aspirational tone, and logo placement [POSITION]. Review the post and tell me specifically what is aligned with our guidelines and what is not. For each issue, explain why it breaks the guideline and give me a concrete fix. Structure the response in two sections: "What works" and "What needs fixing", each as a short bullet list. Keep the language direct, I need to action this quickly.

Key move: giving the exact brand values (hex codes, fonts, rules) makes the review specific instead of generic, and the two section format forces actionable output, not a wall of commentary.

RoleContextTaskFormat
Act as a real estate operations compliance reviewer, expert at auditing submissions, transaction files, and internal documents against company SOPs. I will paste two things: (1) the relevant SOP section for [PROCESS], and (2) the actual document or record submitted by [NAME] for [CASE]. Compare the submission against the SOP step by step. For each required step: completed correctly, completed with errors, or missing. Close with a compliance score as a percentage and a recommendation: approve, return for minor corrections, or escalate. Professional but direct tone.

Key move: pasting both the SOP and the submission gives Claude a clear benchmark, so the output is a side by side gap analysis, not a guess, and the score becomes a trackable metric across the team.

RoleContextTaskFormat
Act as a real estate operations manager preparing the monthly performance report for company leadership. Pasting operational data for [PERIOD]: agent activity metrics, transaction pipeline summary, SOP compliance rates by team, and open escalations. Leadership reviews this every [FREQUENCY] for staffing, process, and training decisions. Facts and recommendations, no filler. Analyze the data and produce the full report: one paragraph executive summary, key metrics by team, what improved versus last period and why, what declined and the likely root cause, process bottlenecks or recurring compliance failures, a staffing or workload observation if the data supports one, and three specific recommendations with rationale. Structured report, labeled sections, plain direct language, no corporate jargon. Recommendations must be concrete ("add a mandatory 48 hour follow up step to the buyer inquiry SOP", not "improve follow up"). Key move: asking for root cause analysis, not just a summary, gets you an operations perspective instead of a spreadsheet recap, and the Green/Amber/Red line gives leadership an instant read, which is how senior decision makers actually consume information.
