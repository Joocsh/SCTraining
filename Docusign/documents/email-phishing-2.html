Training Security Sample — Deceptive Link Destination. Check where the link actually leads!

DocuSign eSignature

NEW DOCUMENT
Display Sender: "Lucas Adminton via DocuSign" <dse@docusign.net>
Actual Header Return-Path: <mailbot@cloudserver-node88.xyz>
To: alex.rivera@skillcloudva.com
Subject: Please DocuSign: Settlement Statement & Escrow Disbursement Approval
Envelope ID: ENV-FAKE-88402 (Unregistered in Manage Tab)

Lucas Adminton at Best Closing Inc. has sent you a settlement statement for approval.

VIEW ENVELOPE

Actual Link Destination: http://185.220.101.5/docusign/verify.html

Phishing Clues for VA: The display text mimics a legitimate sender, but the underlying hyperlink points to a raw IP address (185.220.101.5) instead of a verified *.docusign.net domain. Always verify envelopes inside the DocuSign Manage console directly rather than trusting links in unexpected emails.
